Partner Instructions

Step-by-step guide to using the Thousand Miles partner panel.

Creating a booking Downloading a voucher Cancellations Cancellation policy Contact
1

Creating a booking

1
Go to the Tours tab and select a tour

On the main screen, tap Tours in the top navigation. Browse the available tours and tap the one you want to book.

2
Select a date

A calendar will appear showing available dates. Tap the date of the tour. Days without availability are greyed out.

3
Select a time slot and language (if available)

Depending on the tour, you may see one or more time slots. Some tours offer multiple language options — each slot may represent a different departure time or language of the guide. Tap the slot that matches your guest's preference.

4
Select the number of guests and categories

Use the + and − buttons to set the number of guests in each available category. Common categories are:

  • Adult — standard guest
  • Student — requires valid student ID
  • Child / Infant — age ranges defined per tour
  • Senior — may require proof of age

Categories shown depend on the specific tour — not all tours have all categories.

5
Fill in guest details

Complete the booking form with the following information:

Full name of the lead guest
First and last name of the main person in the group.
Guest email address
The booking confirmation and voucher will be sent to this address.
Phone number
Include the country prefix (e.g. +48 for Poland). Used for day-of-tour contact only.
Payment method
Paid at reception
Guest has already paid at the hotel / reception. Booking is marked as confirmed and paid.
Pay to driver
Guest will pay cash on the day of the tour directly to the guide or driver.
Online (payment link)
A secure payment link is sent to the guest's email. Booking is confirmed once payment is completed.
2

Downloading a voucher

A PDF voucher is available in two ways:

Option A — right after booking

Once the booking is confirmed, a "Print / Save PDF" button appears on screen. Tap it to open and download the voucher immediately.

Option B — from My bookings

Go to the My bookings tab. Find the booking and tap the PDF icon next to it — the voucher will open in a new window ready to print or save.

3

Cancelling a booking

Partners cannot cancel bookings directly in the panel. To request a cancellation, please contact Thousand Miles via the Support tab.

1
Go to the Support tab
Tap Support in the top navigation bar.
2
Select category: "Booking cancellation/issue"
Choose this category from the dropdown.
3
Provide the booking reference
Enter the booking reference number (e.g. TM-453335). You can find it in the My bookings tab.
4
Describe the reason and submit
Briefly explain why the booking needs to be cancelled. Our team will process the request and confirm via the ticket thread.
4

Cancellation policy

Please inform your guests of the following policy
Free cancellation — cancellations made more than 24 hours before the scheduled start of the tour are fully refundable.
No refund — cancellations made less than 24 hours before the tour start time are charged 100% of the tour price.
No refund — bookings made less than 24 hours before the tour start cannot be cancelled and require 100% of the price.
